### ✨ Glass for Patients - Personal AI Health Agent

Glass for Patients is an AI agent designed to assist with personal health management. It processes healthcare data by analyzing it against medical knowledge to help users understand and improve their health.

Key Points:

• Acts as a personal AI agent for health.

• Reviews user healthcare data.

• Analyzes data with global medical knowledge.

• Assists in understanding and optimizing health.
