### 🤖 AI Image Models - Physics-First Un-0

Unconventional AI introduced Un-0, a physics-first image model. This model utilizes simulated coupled oscillators to generate images. It demonstrates a novel approach to image generation.

Key Points:
• Employs a physics-first method for image generation.

• Uses simulated coupled oscillators as its core mechanism.

• Achieves an FID of 6.74 on class-conditional ImageNet 64x64.
