### 🤖 AI Data - Customer Reviews as Training Material

Customer reviews provide a valuable, often overlooked, source of data for artificial intelligence training. This is particularly relevant in specialized sectors like healthcare, wellness, and aesthetics, where specific feedback drives model improvement.

Key Points:
• Customer reviews contribute directly to AI model training datasets.

• This data goes beyond simple social proof, offering structured insights.

• Applications are significant in healthcare, wellness, and aesthetic industries.
