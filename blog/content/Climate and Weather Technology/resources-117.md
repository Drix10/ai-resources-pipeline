### 🤖 Satellite Tracking - Cubesat Telemetry Reception

This article details the process of tracking the Taiwanese cubesat T.Microsat-1, including its transmission characteristics and the equipment used for reception and audio recording.

Key Points:

• T.Microsat-1 transmits at 401.025 MHz.

• The cubesat sends musical notes and telemetry bursts.

• SkyRoof is used for tracking, Audacity for audio recording.

• Reception utilizes an SDRplay RSPduo tuner with a TV Yagi antenna.

🚀 Implementation:
1. Tune SDRplay RSPduo: Configure the tuner to 401.025 MHz.
2. Track Satellite: Use SkyRoof software for orbital tracking.
3. Record Transmissions: Capture audio data with Audacity.
4. Set Up Antenna: Position a TV Yagi antenna for signal reception.
