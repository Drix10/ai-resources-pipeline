## Prompt (verbatim as published)

````text
Create a looping 10s GIF of a Pelican riding a bicycle on a sunny day. The Pelican's basket begins to vibrate, and he picks up a phone as the camera zooms in. We continue zooming in to see the Pelican has been sent, via text message, the exact video we are watching now. The view zooms in on the thumbnail until it becomes the whole screen, and the animation ends, looping back to the beginning.
````
