## Prompt (verbatim as published)

````text
Build a browser-based stop-motion studio with original 3D characters and props. Let people arrange a scene, adjust a character’s pose, capture frames, preview the animation at different speeds, and export a GIF. Include frame reordering, duplication, deletion, and a clear preview of the previous pose. Keep the creative work on a single stage with a simple timeline below it.
````

## Code tab (verbatim as published)

````text
Follow-up prompts in the published build process:

— Create characters and props:
Make the 3D objects more expressive and visually interesting. Add a girl to the character collection and expand the props with plants, furniture, and small scene details. Give the characters distinct silhouettes, clothing, and pose controls that make simple gestures easy to animate.

— Export a short animation:
Create a short sample animation using the characters and props. Capture a sequence of distinct poses, adjust the timing, and export a looping GIF that matches the timeline preview.

— Adapt the workspace to every screen:
Make the side panels resizable without covering the stage on desktop. On mobile, use toggles to open a single overlay panel and switch between Objects and Settings. Keep the timeline and capture controls usable across screen sizes.

— Save projects on a device or account:
Keep automatic saving on this device and make that status clear. Add an optional Sign in with ChatGPT flow so people can save projects to their account and reopen them later. Preserve every frame, object, pose, and timing value.
````
