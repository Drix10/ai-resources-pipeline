# FLOP and GenLayer

Designing AI agents for scale is a paradox: increasing compute power and memory can lead to more disputes, not fewer.

In the pursuit of efficiency, AI developers often overlook the growing problem of disputes between agents.

The root cause of this issue lies in the increasing complexity of AI decision-making processes.

FLOP's probabilistic reasoning mechanism helps to identify and resolve disputes before they escalate.

GenLayer's parallel processing capabilities enable AI agents to operate in real-time.
