# AI/ML - Deep Breakdown

The failure mode in AI model training only shows up under high load, which is why most benchmarks miss it.

As AI model training becomes increasingly complex, it's easy to overlook the importance of a well-designed training pipeline and reward system.

The math behind these models is often opaque, and the training pipeline is often a black box.

But what happens when the model is deployed in production, and the load increases?

The failure mode that was hidden in the training data suddenly becomes apparent.

A deep breakdown of the training pipeline and reward design is crucial to avoid common pitfalls such as overfitting and underfitting.

Overfitting occurs when the model is too complex and fits the training data too well, but fails to generalize to new data.

To avoid these pitfalls, it's essential to run a thorough analysis of the training pipeline to identify potential biases and errors.

This includes examining the data preprocessing steps, the model architecture, and the training algorithm.

It's also essential to use a reward design that balances exploration and exploitation to avoid overfitting.

By following these best practices, you can ensure that your AI model is reliable, accurate, and effective in real-world applications.
